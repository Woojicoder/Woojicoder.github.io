Self-Development Plan 2 · Conflict Management

Focus on shared goals.

My two-week plan to stay in the conversation and work toward a win-win. Tap the hearts to log a session.

Focus area
Collaborating Owl: focus on shared goals, especially with people I'm not close to.
Reason
Teamwork is easy with people I know, but with people I'm not close to I tend to go quiet and go along with what others say. An Owl only gets a win-win by speaking up, and a shared-goal sentence is a safe, easy way to join in.
Goal
With people I'm not close to (new groupmates, classmates, or random teammates online), I'll say one shared-goal sentence at the start, like "What's the goal for this match?" I'll log each time with a 1–5 rating, at least 3 times a week for two weeks.

Part 2

My reflection

What I realized

With friends, speaking up is easy. With people I'm not close to, I tend to stay quiet, and a shared goal can be a simple way in.

How it changed my approach

Naming the shared goal first helps me listen before reacting. Disagreements feel less like me versus them, and more like us solving the same problem.

What I'll keep doing

I'll keep restating the goal in every group task and keep logging it. It's a small habit that will matter in school projects and later in work teams.
